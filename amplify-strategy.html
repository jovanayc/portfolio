02The loop assessment has to serve

Teach → Observe → Assess → Understand → Intervene → Teach.

Traditional assessment stops at a score. The direction here is question → response → interpretation of thinking → instructional recommendation → intervention. [Where you think the loop breaks today.]

03Six customers, one result

This is not normal SaaS. Every assessment decision has to hold up for all of them at once.

  • Student: an accessible, fair experience
  • Teacher: clear, actionable next steps
  • School leader: implementation and reporting
  • District: interoperability, reliability, outcomes
  • State: standards, validity, screening compliance
  • Learning scientists: psychometric validity

05How I think about assessment

01Name the construct. Decide exactly what we are measuring before we write a single question.
02Rubrics are product. A rubric turns a messy human response into a result people can act on.
03AI proposes, people decide. Set quality thresholds and validate before broad release. Humans stay accountable.
04Feedback should teach. A score ends a conversation. A next step starts one.
05Protect integrity. If a result can be gamed, nobody can trust it, least of all the learner.

The loop I design for: question → response → interpretation of thinking → feedback → next practice.
